Talk through the events and templates feature tomorrow.

The core tension: at one point the plan was to just allow **copying events** (e.g. Thanksgiving 2025 → copy → Thanksgiving 2026) rather than a separate templates system. But that makes the existing templates feature feel redundant or confusing.

Questions to work through:
- Does a "copy event" flow replace templates entirely, or do they serve different purposes?
- Templates feel more generic/reusable; copied events carry all the specific history (dates, completed tasks, etc.) — is that a feature or a problem?
- What does the copy UX look like — does it reset task completion, shift dates relative to the new event date, etc.?

**Why:** User raised this as an unresolved design question before going to bed.
**How to apply:** Bring this up proactively at the start of the next session and work through the design before implementing anything.
